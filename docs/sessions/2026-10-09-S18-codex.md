# S18 — The selected O, struck

The owner chose the first O/yellow-bolt study and asked for a thicker bolt that interacts with the O, plus a lightning introduction into an otherwise static O.

## Delivered
- Canonical selected geometry in scripts/strike-mark.mjs: wider gold bolt, full diagonal passage, two crisp ring cuts and angular edge bites. Width at the two intersections is approximately twice the previous study; the ring does not move.
- Static colour and mono SVGs, animated SVG, favicon and wordmarks. The chosen O now matches in wordmark and submark; six other letter outlines are preserved. Original library masters are untouched, and the original S17 first study is archived in public/identity/archive/mark-01-s17.svg.
- One shot CSS/SVG introduction: intact O holds for 700ms, bolt reveals in 160ms, ring cuts at impact, brief glint and two small fragment traces fade, then the final mark holds. No looping or global screen flash. Reduced motion shows the final still immediately.
- Header plays once after its own entrance, with a 1.3s strike delay. Menu/footer stay static, and route navigation retains the header instance. Unique mask IDs prevent header/menu/footer references from colliding.
- /identity/strike.html: replay button, original/revised comparison, colour/reversed/tiny-size samples and SVG downloads. Reversed versions preserve the gold hue. Existing sixteen-study review and download archive updated.
- output/identity-strike: 600×600 GIF (3 seconds, one play), before/impact/final PNGs, preview captures, asset ZIP and README. All created from native SVG/CSS; no raster image generation.
