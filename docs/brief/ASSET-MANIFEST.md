# OLYMPUS asset manifest

Individual, ready-to-place assets for the OLYMPUS creative-agency concept. This is a fictional brand and the selected-work campaign images represent fictional projects. The photographic images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, not photographed in Athens; the brand SVGs were constructed as editable/scalable vector assets. No presentation boards are included.

## Working notes

- Palette: marble `#F4F1EA`; obsidian `#080808`; Zeus gold `#D4AF37`; Aegean blue `#2563EB`; Apollo orange `#FF6A00`.
- Prefer SVG for the logo and marks. Do not typeset a new wordmark in the site header.
- Keep page copy and UI controls in HTML, not burned into hero photographs.
- The Greek text `ΟΛΥΜΠΟΣ` is a subtle symbolic nod, not a substitute English name.
- `CLAUDE-WEBSITE-PROMPT.txt` is the copy-paste-ready website brief. `IMAGE-GENERATION-PROMPTS.md` records the image direction and asset prompts.
