VISUAL SYSTEM
Ancient authority × contemporary creative culture. Classical sculpture collides with modern editorial design; museum permanence with irreverent mark-making. Base palette: marble #F4F1EA and obsidian #080808. Accents: Zeus gold #D4AF37, Aegean blue #2563EB, Apollo orange #FF6A00. Use blue/orange/gold decisively and sparingly. The palette should breathe: mostly ivory, black and stone.

Typography: an oversized blunt geometric grotesk (Archivo, Space Grotesk, or an equally apt open font), an elegant high-contrast editorial serif (Instrument Serif, Cormorant Garamond, or equivalent), and very occasional handwritten annotation. Do not use Greek-key borders. Build a 12-column editorial desktop grid, 6–8 columns tablet, and a deliberately composed 4-column mobile system. Embrace asymmetry, hard crops, enormous but controlled type, visual pauses, narrow text columns, full-bleed imagery, crop/registration details, and unexpected changes of scale. Give every major section a distinct compositional idea while making the whole feel authored by one studio.
