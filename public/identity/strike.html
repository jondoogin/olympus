The O, struck.

A broader yellow bolt. Two precise fractures. An intact O holds still, the strike arrives, and the finished mark remains.

Force leaves a mark.

The bolt cuts through the ring at both contact points. Small angular breaks give the O a reaction to the strike; the silhouette stays clean enough for a favicon or a stamp.

The introduction plays once: a quiet hold, a fast diagonal strike, a brief glint, then a still mark.
